Hi, I’m V.

I made Letter from V as a place to keep the things that feel too meaningful to disappear into a camera roll or a passing thought.

I write about life in New York, places I want to remember, beauty, travel, relationships, and whatever else I’m thinking about long enough to turn into words.

This is not meant to be a perfectly curated life. It is simply a collection of letters from wherever I happen to be.

With love, V
